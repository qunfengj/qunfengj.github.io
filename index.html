I study how planetary systems form and evolve—and what their present-day orbits remember.

My work combines N-body dynamics, statistical inference, and observations to connect individual systems with exoplanet populations.

Questions, systems, and current manuscripts

I work across scales—from the architecture of a single nearby system to demographic structures in thousands of planets.

How do irradiation histories shape short-period planets?

Comparing the Neptunian-desert boundary across M dwarfs and FGK stars using stellar activity histories, global boundary models, and statistical null experiments.

Published in The Astronomical Journal, 172, 306 (2026)

What architectures can survive in π Mensae?

Combining 772 published radial velocities with three-dimensional N-body integrations to constrain candidate planet d and test secular excitation by the outer giant.

Accepted for publication in The Astronomical Journal

Why are circumbinary planets dynamically cold?

Measuring planetary free eccentricities with N-body integrations and Fourier analysis, then using K–S comparisons to propose a stability-filter hypothesis for the observed population.

Revised and resubmitted to The Astrophysical Journal

Can lower-mass giants migrate without clearing the inside?

REBOUND and REBOUNDx integrations with tidal calculations test whether high-eccentricity migration can preserve close inner planets.

In preparation with Juliette Becker

Tidal systematics before third bodies

Target-specific stellar models remove the claimed apsidal-motion excesses for CH Ind and SW CMa.

A challenge to early in-situ formation

Dynamical tests of whether the small satellites could have formed early at their present locations.

Eccentricity across period and multiplicity

Hierarchical models of transit-derived posteriors for super-Earth and sub-Neptune populations.
