I grew up in Jiangxi, a province in southern China, before moving to Shanghai for my undergraduate studies in physics at Fudan University. During and after my undergraduate years, I spent two periods as a visiting researcher in California, working on problems across astrophysics and Earth observation. These experiences led me to pursue an MPhil in Planetary Sciences in Hong Kong — picking up Cantonese along the way.
